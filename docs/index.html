How Game Crate works

Playing

  • Tap a cartridge to see it, then tap it again or press Play.
  • Use the genre tabs at the top to filter, or Search to find a game by name.
  • In a game, tap the house button on the right side to come back here. You can drag it out of the way.
  • On a computer, the keyboard tab on the left side of each game lists its controls.

Below the shelf

  • Scroll down for achievements, picks for you, favorites, the daily challenge, your stats and more. The buttons under the genres jump straight to each one.
  • Tap the heart next to Play to add a game to Favorites.
  • Daily challenge: just play that day's game before midnight. You do not have to beat it. A new one shows up every day.
  • Earn achievements on the console and inside each game. A notification pops up in the game the moment you get one.
  • Earn achievements to unlock new menu colors in Themes.

Saving and reset

  • Your progress saves on this device by itself, even when you refresh or close the app.
  • Only Settings (the gear) can reset it. If you set a reset code, nobody can reset without it. Your phone can make the code and save it in the Passwords app.
  • Saves inside each game stay separate and are never touched by a reset.
