Controls

SteerDrag your finger left and right. Arrow keys work on a computer.

Points

DistanceYou score just by staying alive. The road speeds up the farther you go.
★ Stars+10 each.
PIT+50. Pull alongside a car in an outer lane and swipe into it from the inside to knock it off the road. Works on vans too.
DifficultyEasy ×0.5, Normal ×1, Hard ×2 points.
StreakPlay on back-to-back days for +10% points per day, up to +50%. 3 and 7 day streaks unlock skins.

Ways to die

Armor ▰▰▰You have 3. Rear-ending a car or van, or side-swiping one in a middle lane, costs 1. Lose all 3 and you're scrap.
MinesSpiked discs with a blinking red light. Cost 1 armor.
FuelDrains the whole time. Grab red gas cans to refill. Run dry and it's over.
Oil slicksNormal and Hard. Glowing puddles that spin you out for a second with no steering.
Lane changersCars with a flashing blinker are about to move over.
3-car wallsHard only. One gap, close to the last one.

Events

Every 20–30 seconds one hits for 8 seconds. A gold banner and timer show which.

Rush hourTraffic packs in tighter.
Star rushStars in every open lane.
MinefieldA mine almost every row.
Double pointsDistance, stars and PITs pay double.
Fog bankFog covers the top of the road. You only see what's close.

Missions

3 new missions every day. Each one done earns 1 medal, and finishing all 3 earns 2 more. Medals unlock the Stealth, Toxic and Royal skins.

Zones

The scenery changes every 450 m: pines, desert, city, snow, then back around.

Drag to dodge traffic and mines. Grab gas cans or you run dry. Side-swipe a car in an outer lane to PIT it off the road for points.
